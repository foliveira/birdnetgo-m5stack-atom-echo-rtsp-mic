Audio

Adjusts gain to the level of the scene on top of the gain above. Off gives BirdNET a constant level.

WiFi and power

Lets the radio sleep between beacons, the largest heat saving. TCP then gets acknowledgements only once per beacon, which is enough for 16 kHz with an access point DTIM period of 1 but not for higher rates. Switched off automatically if the stream drops frames.

Protection and maintenance

Stops the RTSP server after 3 consecutive minutes at or above the limit, until you resume it here.
